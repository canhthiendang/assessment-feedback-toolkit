## Visible introduction

### Create clearer assessment guidance and more useful feedback

Use this tool before an assessment to create a student-facing Assessment and Feedback Guide, or after an assessment to create a cohort-level General Feedback document for KEATS.

Start from your syllabus with ChatGPT or complete a short structured form yourself. Expandable examples are available throughout, so you can copy, adapt, and move on rather than start from a blank page.

**Indicative time:** approximately 5–10 minutes with a well-prepared syllabus and the ChatGPT-assisted route, or 10–20 minutes using the manual route. A General Feedback document should take approximately 10–15 minutes once the module team has identified the main cohort-level themes. These are pilot estimates and will vary by module.

Every Word document is fully editable. Nothing is published automatically. You retain academic ownership and responsibility for reviewing, adapting, and approving the final content.

## Expandable: Why is this useful?

Students are more likely to engage confidently when they understand what an assessment is designed to evaluate, what good performance looks like, how to prepare, and how to use feedback. The tool provides a consistent starting point while preserving the flexibility needed for different economics and management assessments.

## Expandable: What remains under my control?

You decide which route to use, what information to include, and whether to use or rewrite any example. The output is an editable Word document. The tool does not publish to KEATS, email colleagues, or make academic decisions on your behalf.

## Expandable: How is my information used?

In the manual route, the form content and selected filename remain in your browser. In the ChatGPT-assisted route, you upload the syllabus to your own ChatGPT conversation; this website does not receive your syllabus, ChatGPT conversation, or ChatGPT credentials. Do not use identifiable student information or upload student work.

If you separately choose anonymous usage reporting, the site will send only the faculty, department, document type, completion route, assessment profile where relevant, and date/academic period. This allows Dr Canh Thien Dang to report aggregated use of the tool. It will not send your name, email, module details, syllabus, responses, feedback content, generated document, or ChatGPT content. Declining usage reporting will not affect your access to the tool.
